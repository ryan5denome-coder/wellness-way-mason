# All agents share ONE group on purpose. Under RFC 9309 a crawler obeys only
# the most specific group that names it, so a bot listed in its own group
# would ignore Disallow rules written under "User-agent: *". Keeping every
# named bot in the same group as "*" means the private paths apply to all.
