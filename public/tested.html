Where the two-loss number comes from

You may already qualify

ACOG"Recurrent pregnancy loss is defined as having two or more miscarriages. After two miscarriages, a thorough physical exam and testing are recommended."
ASRMEvaluation may proceed after two losses — a position first stated in 2012. Updated in 2026: a pregnancy confirmed by a positive hCG test counts, and losses no longer need to be consecutive.
Your insurerCoverage policies commonly define it at two or more losses. Denials usually come from a plan-level fertility exclusion or a coding issue — both worth appealing.
In the UKRCOG still says three or more. Your doctor is following their guideline, not ignoring one.

Your side

What we run, and why

Grouped by what each one is actually looking at. Not a protocol — a set of inputs we can measure.

Inflammation & immune load

CRP, ESR, CMP, and thyroid antibodies (TPO).

A normal TSH does not rule out an immune system attacking the thyroid. TSH is a pituitary signal; antibodies are a separate test that often goes unordered.

Nutrient status

Full iron panel + CBC, vitamin D, magnesium.

A ferritin of 15 isn't anemia, so it gets flagged normal — and it's nowhere near where you'd want it going into a pregnancy.

Blood sugar

Fasting insulin, glucose, HbA1c, lipids.

Fasting insulin is the one most often skipped. Insulin climbs years before glucose does — a normal glucose with a high fasting insulin is a picture you'd otherwise miss entirely.

Oxidative stress & toxic load

OAT (organic acids) and MycoTOX.

Metabolic and mitochondrial markers, plus mold and mycotoxin exposure. Mold testing is contested in conventional medicine; we measure it, and we don't claim it explains a loss.

Gut & estrogen clearance

Genova 3-day stool.

Digestion, absorption, overgrowths, gut inflammation — and how well you clear the estrogen you've already used. Hormones aren't only made; they have to leave.

The cycle itself

DUTCH Cycle Mapping.

Estrogen and progesterone across the whole cycle rather than one morning — whether you ovulated, when, how strongly, and what progesterone did afterward. A standard day-21 draw can miss the peak entirely if you ovulate late.

His side

Half the equation, usually unmeasured

Half the genetic material is his — and that half gets built by his body, out of whatever his body has to work with. In most workups, nothing on his side was ever looked at.

ASRM, 2026: "Standard semen analysis parameters do not appear to be predictive of recurrent pregnancy loss." So a normal semen analysis isn't the all-clear it gets treated as.

  • Full thyroid panel — men get thyroid problems too, and almost nobody screens them in a fertility context
  • Fasting insulin & A1c — a lot of otherwise fit men are insulin resistant. It's a number, and it moves.
  • Genova 3-day stool — "I can eat anything" isn't proof the gut is fine
  • OAT — mitochondrial and oxidative load
  • MycoTOX — usually a shared household exposure, so worth checking both of you
The honest part. We can't tell you that sorting out his thyroid or his blood sugar changes what happens next. Nobody can honestly tell you that. What we can tell you is that half of this has never been measured.
Not our lane

What we don't run — and who does

We'd rather say this plainly than have you find out later.

TestWhere to ask
Semen analysis & sperm DNA fragmentationYour OB, an REI, or a reproductive urologist.
Karyotype — both partnersYour OB or an REI. A balanced translocation is real and findable.
Tissue (POC) chromosome analysisYour OB, at the time of a loss. ASRM now recommends this as a first step — ask before tissue is discarded.
Endometrial biopsyYour OB or an REI. Silent chronic inflammation of the lining is only found by looking.
Saline sonogram / hysteroscopyYour OB. A septum can be missed on a standard ultrasound.
Celiac diagnosisYour GP or a gastroenterologist. Don't go gluten-free before testing — it invalidates the result.

Before you pay anyone

What we will not claim

You've likely been sold certainty before, and it cost money and time you don't get back. So, plainly:

  • We will not tell you MTHFR caused your losses. The evidence isn't there.
  • We will not tell you levothyroxine prevents miscarriage in women with normal thyroid function and positive antibodies. A randomized trial found it didn't.
  • We will not sell you NK cell testing, intralipids, or hair mineral analysis as fertility diagnostics.
  • We will not tell you stress caused this.
  • We will never promise you a baby. Nobody honestly can.
What we will do. Tell you exactly what we test and why. Tell you what each result would change. And tell you when we've found nothing — out loud, without softening it into a supplement plan.

Some of what we find can be changed. Some can't. We'll tell you which.
