What the talk covers

Inflammation is a response, not a diagnosis

Joint pain, gut trouble, skin flare-ups, fatigue, stubborn weight, brain fog. Very different symptoms, one common thread: the body responding to something it is being asked to deal with. This talk is about finding that something instead of quieting the response.

  • What inflammation actually is, and why "chronic" inflammation shows up in so many different ways
  • The three sources of stress on the body that The Wellness Way looks at first: traumas, toxins, and thoughts
  • Why two people with the same symptoms can have completely different causes
  • Test, don't guess: which labs help you see what is driving inflammation for you
  • Time for your questions

Plain honesty. This is an educational evening, not a treatment plan. Nothing said from the stage is a diagnosis, and our care works alongside your medical providers, never in place of them.
