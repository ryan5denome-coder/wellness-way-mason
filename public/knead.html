Meet the bakers

Behemoth Bakery

No Gluten. No Compromise. All Flavor.

Behemoth is a small gluten-free bakery from Fort Thomas, Kentucky, baking with whole grains, superfoods, and clean ingredients. Instead of wheat, they use flours like millet, sorghum, brown rice, and tapioca, plus psyllium and apple cider vinegar.

Their lineup has included ciabatta, focaccia, Italian herbed focaccia, cinnamon raisin swirl, banana bread, and The Pumpkin King, along with cookies, tarts, and pies.

Good to know

Questions

Do I need to be gluten-free to come?

Not at all. Come if you've already cut gluten, if you're thinking about it, or if you just love good bread.

Is everything gluten-free?

Behemoth bakes gluten-free. If you have celiac disease or a serious food allergy, call us at (283) 223-8376 before the event and we'll get you ingredient details.

What does the $10 ticket include?

Your seat, every tasting and pairing, and the talk and Q&A with Dr. Ryan.

Can I bring a friend?

Please do. Each guest needs their own ticket so we bake enough bread for everyone.

How do tickets work?

Tickets are sold through Luma. You'll get a confirmation email right away and a reminder before the event.
