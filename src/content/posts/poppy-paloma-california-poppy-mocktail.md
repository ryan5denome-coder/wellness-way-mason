## A festive drink without the alcohol

A good mocktail should taste like it belongs at the party, not like a compromise. The Poppy Paloma does that with fresh grapefruit juice, lime, a lightly sweetened white tea, and a salted citrus rim. The sparkling grapefruit juice (or sparkling water) at the end is what makes it feel like a Paloma.

This recipe makes several glasses, so it works for a small get-together or a quiet night at home. If you're hosting, you can steep the tea and juice the fruit ahead of time, then shake and top with bubbles when people arrive.

## A little about California poppy

California poppy (*Eschscholzia californica*) is a yellow-orange wildflower, and it's a different plant from the opium poppy. The Wellness Way's article [California Poppy: When Stress Threatens to Take You Out, Poppy Brings Peace and Calm](https://www.thewellnessway.com/california-poppy-when-stress-threatens-to-take-you-out-poppy-brings-peace-and-calm/) describes it as a traditional medicinal plant of Native American communities on the West Coast. According to that article, California poppy has long been brewed into teas and tinctures, and it has traditionally been used to support relaxation, soothe a sense of stress, and support restful sleep.

Today, many people use The Wellness Way California Poppy Liquid Herb in that same traditional way, to support relaxation. That's the wording we use on purpose. We are not making medical claims about this herb, and a mocktail is not a care plan. It's a festive drink that includes a house herbal product you can order from our office.

A special drink can make a gathering feel more relaxed. It can't do all the work of a full month. ## Poppy Paloma recipe

**Ingredients**

- 3/4 cup water
- 2 white tea bags
- 1 cup fresh grapefruit juice
- Zest of 1/4 grapefruit
- 1/2 cup fresh lime juice
- 1/4 cup coarse sea salt
- 3 tablespoons honey
- 1 (12 ounce) can or bottle of sparkling grapefruit juice or sparkling water
- 2 teaspoons The Wellness Way California Poppy Liquid Herb

**Directions**

1. Boil the water and steep the tea bags for 2 minutes.
2. Remove the tea bags, gently squeezing them out.
3. Add 2 tablespoons of the honey and stir until dissolved.
4. Place the tea in the fridge to cool.
5. Zest 1/4 of the grapefruit, then add 1/4 cup of salt to the zest and mix well.
6. Spread a little of the remaining honey on the rim of each glass, then dip it in the zest-and-salt mixture.
7. Juice the grapefruit and limes, and strain the pulp with a strainer or cheesecloth.
8. In a shaker glass, combine the fresh grapefruit juice, lime juice, cooled white tea, and California poppy.
9. Add ice to the drink glasses.
10. Fill each prepared glass about halfway with the grapefruit, lime, white tea, and poppy mixture.
11. Top off with sparkling grapefruit juice or sparkling water.

A few practical notes: fresh juice tastes brighter than bottled juice in this recipe, and straining the pulp keeps the drink clear. If you'd rather skip the salted rim, the mocktail still tastes great without it. For a less sweet glass, use sparkling water and start with a little less honey in the tea.
